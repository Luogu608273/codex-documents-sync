    No.434 ピアノポップ練習曲第1番「片晶」
    Personal Score: 980 ( Music 90 & Chart 90 & Visual 0 / out of 100 )

    [Music]
    It's a interesting idea to make an etude with techniques in pop piano with a pop drum set.
    The development becomes attractive when the chords coming from parallel major and minor together.
    Some blues notes appeared rarely also matches the idea of pop.

    [Chart]
    [SPH] has some jacks that makes the chart a bit difficult, such as in Bar 34 and Bar 72, while they fit the drums though.
    [SPB] is fine, but the gap between [SPB] and [SPH] is too large from LV2 to LV10.
    [SPA] is a great chart which fully shows the flow of piano, but some keys for drums are a bit strange.

    [Visual]
    It's a pity that no BGA has been made, not even a single BGI.

    感谢创作。
